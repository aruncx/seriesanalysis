Why faculty use it

Built for the way a department reviews series tests: one result sheet in, decisions and documents out.

Private by design

It runs entirely in your browser. Result files are never uploaded to a server.

Reads the college report

Load the Series Test Analysis Report as it is. Subjects, faculty, maximum marks, attendance, batch and semester are picked up automatically.

Series I, II and III compared

See who improved, declined, cleared a failure or failed something new, by subject and by student.

Support, with reasons

Configurable rules flag students who need follow-up and state why, with High priority, Priority and Monitor labels that describe follow-up, not ability.

Editable remedial plans

Interventions, mentors, review dates and status stay under faculty control and carry into the exports.

Mentors and electives

Allocate students to mentors by roll-number ranges, and treat elective subjects correctly.

Print-ready PDF

A4 landscape report with a cover, executive summary, charts, colour-coded tables, progress cards and a page preview. Choose the sections and their order.

Styled Excel workbook

Coloured sheets with filters, frozen headers and status colours for record keeping and further analysis.

Free and simple

How it works

Four steps from upload to report.

  1. Load the resultsAdd the Series Test I and II files (.xls, .xlsx or .csv). Series III is optional.
  2. Confirm the settingsCheck the department, subjects, core or elective type, maximum and pass marks, and add mentors if needed.
  3. Review and editRead the analysis, comparison, support list and remedial plan, and edit anything that needs a faculty decision.
  4. ExportPreview and download the PDF report and the Excel workbook from the Export centre.

Rules at a glance

Absent counts as a fail. The default pass mark is 40% of the maximum and can be changed per subject. Pass % is passed divided by enrolled students. Blank marks are never treated as zero. A student is called "Improved" only when no new failure appears. Please verify important decisions against the official records.
