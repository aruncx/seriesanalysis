Compare series tests and plan support

Load Series I, II and III result files, compare them, find students who need academic support, plan remedial action, then export a designed PDF and a multi-sheet Excel workbook. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Data and settings

Result files (.xlsx, .xls or .csv, one row per student)

The college “Series Test Analysis Report” export (.xls) loads directly: subject names, faculty, maximum marks, batch, semester and date are read from its header block. For other files use the columns Roll No, Student ID, Univ Reg No, Name, then one column per subject. Use AB for absent. Blank marks are never treated as zero. An optional “Failures” column is cross-checked against computed failures.
